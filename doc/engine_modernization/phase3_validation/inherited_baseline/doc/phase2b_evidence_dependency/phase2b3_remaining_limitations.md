# Phase 2B.3 remaining correction #6 limitations

## Fixed logical defect

The generic two-strong-indicator diversity check now requires eligible
measurement-disjoint witnesses. One observation and its descendants cannot
satisfy this source requirement by receiving different module/domain labels.
Unknown or missing upstream lineage cannot silently count as another source.
Unsupported affirmative independence wording is removed, and production traces
explain source diversity separately from compound agreement.

This fixes a specific structural corroboration defect. It does not establish
statistical independence or solve every form of correlated evidence weighting.
The general-confidence fallback can change without changing a numerical score.

## Intentionally unresolved numerical issues

- Status/domain sums still award the existing 0.25 domain diversity bonus.
  Several domains may share measurements. Replacing domain credit with family
  budgets would be a new numerical model, prohibited in this task.
- Local/edit, sensor/compression and material/texture/asymmetry synergies retain
  all existing weights and multi-role effects. Exact arithmetic reconciliation
  does not demonstrate calibrated incremental information.
- Camera compatibility still affects benign bases, damping, cautions, local
  tempering and clone moderation. Their combined statistical strength needs
  labeled validation rather than a structural deletion rule.
- The “synthetic support from other domains” field is an OR support flag, not a
  proof of independent corroboration. Material can appear both in the compound
  and in that flag. Requiring a new third source would redefine the intended
  compound model, so the current predicate is explicitly classified as
  accumulation and retained.
- Global/regional spectra and surface metrics can have disjoint extraction IDs
  while sharing content, feature definitions and preprocessing. Disjoint IDs are
  only a structural diversity witness; actual covariance and redundancy remain
  unmeasured.

## Compound mechanisms are not an independence loophole

Boundary contour interpretation, map intensity localization, person-mask
alignment, intrinsic edge-ring comparisons and patch topology can add useful
mechanism information even when related. They remain available under explicit
compound semantics, without a claim of independent measurement confirmation.

Their existing fixed route tiers remain unchanged. Therefore not every High
route requires two disjoint measurement witnesses: that requirement belongs to
the corrected generic diversity gate, not every compound detector model. Any
proposal to impose a universal multi-family rule requires a separately defined
evidentiary purpose and benchmark validation.

Summary-only APIs may omit raw parent summaries while providing observed child
interpretations. These are marked provisional and cannot count as distinct
sources. A compound condition can retain its historical interpretation-level
behavior; the trace states this explicitly. No missing parent measurement is
invented to make the source-diversity gate pass.

## Geometric and feature-contract limitations

- Intrinsic subject-boundary rings are image-edge-derived and are not proven to
  coincide with the selected person contour merely because a person mask exists.
- Boundary-specific strength values retain heterogeneous units. Their existing
  maximum comparison is preserved as part of a compound gate; standardizing it
  would require separately scoped feature/interpretation validation.
- The mask-morphology stage remains unscored in normal section synthesis, with
  the existing snapshot alias to region masks. Connecting it would introduce a
  new scoring interpretation and is not included.
- Registry availability is based on current summary/parent contracts. Future
  detectors should expose explicit feature availability when an active summary
  does not imply the underlying extraction occurred.

## Validation still required

Two selected reference images and synthetic regressions demonstrate code
behavior, not forensic accuracy, false-positive rate or confidence calibration.
Further work needs source-separated labeled authentic/computational/social-
recompressed/conventionally edited/AI-enhanced/generated/local-insertion cohorts,
multiple camera/generator/processing families, and independently varied explicit,
stripped and forged metadata. Keep root variants and near-duplicates in the same
evaluation split.

Measure incremental value conditional on parents, per-hypothesis errors,
coverage/abstention and tier reliability. Evaluate resizing/recompression and
mask quality strata. Do not infer that lower diagnostic scores or the controlled
High -> Medium change are automatically more accurate. No probabilities or
empirical independence estimates are introduced here.

Phase 2B.2 default and experimental shadow policies remain diagnostic. The
audit-A scores are not activated or compared with production thresholds.
Empirical numerical weighting/calibration requires a separate task and approval;
this phase does not attempt correction #9 or alter the general hypothesis-
selection architecture.
