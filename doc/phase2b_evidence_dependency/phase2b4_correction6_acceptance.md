## Resolution of the original F01 examples

The prior audit identified confirmed computational overlap and unreconciled
numerical paths; it explicitly did not prove that every child contribution was
wholly redundant. This reassessment examines the actual additional properties
rather than adopting the diagnostic downstream-removal ablation.

- Cross plus **fragment fallback** is a confirmed category-B repeat. All three
  positive fragment contribution paths and its extra weighted domain are now
  withheld when cross already represents the overlap.
- Cross plus **positive contour boundary**, focus and patch spatial are category
  C relationships: connected contour geometry, regional intensity and endpoint
  topology are separately measured additions. Their original contributions and
  explicit compound conditions remain. They are not extra independent sources.
- Local-support conversion appears once in the final edit term. Local support
  is not also added directly to the suspicious total. The redundant fragment
  input is removed before conversion; no second aggregate subtraction occurs.
- Other single-signal multi-role weights, domain categories for retained genuine
  findings, surface carry-forward and camera/provenance moderation have explicit
  existing scoring roles or uncertain conditional value. No identical numerical
  operation or demonstrably information-free representation remains identified
  in those reviewed paths. Their effectiveness is an empirical question, not
  permission to invent a discount or delete every shared-parent contribution.

On this evidence, no **known category A/B numerical defect identified in the
review remains uncorrected**. Full acceptance depends on these current source
contracts; a future detector or changed classifier must revisit its equivalence
contract and add appropriate regression coverage. The acceptance does not mean
that arbitrary independently copied, unregistered summaries have authenticated
observation identity: lacking a source contract, equality is insufficient to
erase potentially distinct measurements.
